And lastly I do hereby nominate, constitute and appoint my two sons, Martin S. Overholt and Christian S. Overholt and my nephew, Jacob O. Tinstman to be my Executors to this my last will and testament, to whom I give and bequeath three per centum out of my Estate for their time, trouble and expenses in settling my estate and doing all the business pertaining thereto as directed by me in every respect.

1 witness, I Abraham Overholt, the Testator, have to this my last will and Testiment[sic], written on three half sheets of paper set my hand and seal this sixteenth day of November, one thousand eight hundred and sixty three. Abraham Overholt (SEAL)

Signed,sealed and published by the Testator as his last will and testament in our presence, and we have signed the same as witness at his request and in his presence and in the presence of each other.

Abraham Overholt

John O. Overholt

Westmoreland County: As

I, Clark F. Warden, Register of Wills,etc. (SEAL) in and for said County, do certify that I affixed the Probate of the original Will of Abraham Overholt, deceased, remaining of file in my office and of which the foregoing is a true copy. A $200.00 U.S. Int. Rev. Stamp and canceled the same this day, the value of the property passing under said will having been declared to exceed Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($400,000.00) and not to exceed $450,000.00. Witness my hand and seal of office this 26th day of January A.D. 1870.

Clark F. Warden

Register, etc.
