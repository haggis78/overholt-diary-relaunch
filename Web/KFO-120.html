Uncle Clay took Uncle Edgar to the P.W.C. at Chester, Penna. For a year of schooling. Edgar]had been working for about a year at Broadford, and after his year at P.W.C, he returned and clerked at Valley, then Dunbar, and then again at Broadford. In spring of 1880 he left Bfd and went to Wooster with his parents.
