Matin O.
—Born 1709 30 mi from F. on M.
Settled 1725 Bedminster Twp, Bucks Co, Penna.
Married 1736, Nov. 2, to Agnes….(5 children)
Died 1744, Apr. 5, & buried Mennonite graveyard, Deep Run, Bucks Co, Pa.
Henry O.
----born 1739, Feb.5, Bucks Co, Pa.
Married to Anna Beitler (12 children)
Sold 1800 his 175 a farm Bucks Co, and moved to West Overton, Wmld. Co.
Died 1813, Mar 5- & buried Men. Cem. Alverton, Wmld. Co.
