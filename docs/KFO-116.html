Will not wonder that the children and grandchildren feel a sorrow so deep and the loss so irreparable.

They carried her in theMt. Pleasant cemetery,by the side of her loved ones soon to be followed by some of her loved ones, there together to wait our Lord’s return.

Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord, from henceforth, yea, saith the Spirit, tht they may rest from their labors & their works do follow them.

L.S.
