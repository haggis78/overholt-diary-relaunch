June 1868

78 June 1868

Uncle Clay - who had clerked ^ 1st at Eatons for $6.00 a week, 2 at Macrum & Carlisle’s for 8.00 a week, located on 5th st. now 219 Fifth Ave in Pgh- came home ill with intermittent fever, & occupied the N.E. corner room looking toward Mt P (which Mother later used as her room)

He had several sets of sugar coated pills brought home from Pgh. Dr. J. McConaughy of Mt Pl- father of Rober M McConaughy attended him, and he was ill a couple of months.

Mother acted as nurse, brining him water from the spring, reading the New Testament to him as he wished. Sermon on Mount.

She gave him GdM’s as he cared so much for them.
