[Page 34]

Mrs. Law tells of A.O. sending her father C.SO. (his son) out to buy up wheat in Ohio, as he felt that the wheat crop would be short that year, and that prices would be put up, & people generally would suffer from lack.

Each son went to different places, & much was bought & when they returned they thought perhaps A.O. would say they brought too much. However, instead of that, A.O. told them to start out again the next day & “buy as much more.” He then accumulated a great deal, and the expected shortage occurred. The people flocked to him, wanting wheat & many wanted moor [sic] than they actually needed, & ^some were willing to pay high for it.

However, he sold to each enough for their actually needs, but not more; also he sold at what it cost him, not boosting the price.
