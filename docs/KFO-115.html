The following is copy of newspaper clipping:

In Memorian

"The following tribute to the memory of Mrs. Overholt, recently deceased, has been written by one well-acquainted with her for a long time:

The parents of the deceased, Mr and Mrs Stauffer, [Abraham Stauffer and Anna Nissley Stauffer]moved from Lancaster Co, Penna, to Fayette County, near what is now Fountain Mills, during the last century, where Maria was born on July 13, 1791.

At the age of 18 she married Abraham Overholt- with whom she lived over 60 years, he having died Jany 15, 1870. 

They began housekeeping in a little stone building, which is still standing near the homestead from which she has just taken her final departure- making 65 years she lived on that farm. About the time of her marriage, she and her husband united with the Mennonite Church, of which they remained members during life.

She had 8 children, of whom only 3 survive her. At the time of her death, she had 48 grandchildren, & 25 great grandchildren.

She retained remarkable activity of body & mind to within a few months of her death; her mind remaining remarkably clear to the last. She sat up on last Sabbath, Nov. 1, and conversed but was very weak. That Sabbath became her eternal Sabbath.

Her tenderness of hearth, peace of mind, & abiding faith seemed to increase & brighten as her strength failed.

As the time of her departure drew near, it became easy to say "The Lord’s Will, and not mine.” Those who remember that sweet face, and knew that loving heart, and heard that winning voice,

Will not wonder that the children and grandchildren feel a sorrow so deep and the loss so irreparable.

They carried her in theMt. Pleasant cemetery,by the side of her loved ones soon to be followed by some of her loved ones, there together to wait our Lord’s return.

Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord, from henceforth, yea, saith the Spirit, tht they may rest from their labors & their works do follow them.

L.S.
