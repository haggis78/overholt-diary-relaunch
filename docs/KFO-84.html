84

In 1855 Gf.F. took Uncle Clay a little over 5, with him to Van Buren,O, to see his father Daniel Frick.

Uncle Clay became ill with fever, and he & Gf.F. were away two months.

During their absence, Gm Frick lived with her father A.O, at West Overton, and Uncle Aaron was born there 4/7/55.

Sept 20, ‘55, Gt Gf Daniel Frick died at Van Buren.

Ind 1856, Gf & Gm F. moved to a farm just west of Mt. Pleasant, known as the Jacob S. Overholt farm.

In 1857 & 1858 they lived at the Bixler farm, opposite Old Independent School. Here Uncle Clay attended school 2 semesters.
