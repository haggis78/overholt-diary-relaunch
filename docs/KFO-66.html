Mother's ridinghorse was called “Stockings.”

He was a grey, and Mother rode him to Mt. Pleasant to do her shopping.

She rode a great deal, and still has her saddle.

She and a number of girls rode to Greensburg- 18 miles- and took part in a street parade during a presidential campaign dressed in white & on ^the white (grey) horse.

The boys & girls wold have riding parties, the boys carrying on their arms picnic lunch baskets.

After Cousin Mollie Overholt married Capt. Cassius Marklel in 1865, & went to West Newton to live, her sister Maria T. O of Mt Pleasant (then of Broadford) & mother would ride horseback to West Newton to visit.

“Stockings” - a bay mare, saddles only, was one of two horses (bought by JSRO at Frankport, Ky for 250$ & was sold to Gf A.O. for 500$ who gave her to his granddaughter M. O. F>

She was a beauty and gentle, but John Lowstettler, a feeble boy who tended the horses, abused her and she became nervous & wild. Once Mother was coming home from shopping in Mt. P, & Sue Felgar was along & made her horse go, using tongue movement; that excited Stockings & Mother had to run her into a corner & quiet her down. She never rode her after that.
