Mother born Feby 9th, 1848 at Abraham Overholt homestead in West Overton, Pa.

2nd floor S.W. room

Dr. James McConaughey officiated

Uncle Clay born Dec. 19th, 1849 at cottage, Abraham Overholt homestead in West Overton, Pa.

Miss Kate Sherrick related how her grandmother, Mrs. Joseph Sherrick was in the smoke-house on her own place, one late afternoon when “Grandpap O” rode by on his horse, on his way to fetch Dr. James McCanaughy [sic].

He stopped at the smoke house & leaning from his horse tapped on the window to tell Mrs. Sherrick, who dropped everything and hurried over.
