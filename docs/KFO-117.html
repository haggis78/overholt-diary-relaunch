On Nov. 1874, Gdm.O.died from dropsy & old age (83 ⅓ yrs). The acute trouble first appeared in her heal. Her limbs & body became much swollen, and she suffered a great deal, being conscious to the end. Mother also Father were there for a week prior to the end.  

None of the Stauffer family were there, so far as Mother recalls. After death custom.

Mother had always returned to West Overton twice a year, after marriage to visit her Grmd & generally stayed two weeks at a time.

Seeing Clay there?

Nov 19 & 20, 1874, sale at West Overton of Gdm O's properties by Martin S. O admin. Uncle Clay present
