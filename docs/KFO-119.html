April 17, 1875, Jay [Overholt] was born at the Beall Avenue property. Dr {blank} officiated. 

{note in margin: see HCF[ Henry Clay Frick] day book page 49} June 19, 1875, -Uncle Clay visited them while at the Beall Ave. property. Mr Andrew W Mellon was with him. They were there two days.

Augus 1875- F & M moved into their new home, #23 Spink Street, which cost $2600.00. The lot cost $775.00, making the total cost $3375.00. Robert Cameron was the builder.

{note in margin: See HCF day book p 89} December 1875- Uncle Clay visited F & M in their new home.
